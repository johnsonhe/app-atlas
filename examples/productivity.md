# Subscription and Paywall Comparison: Notion, Evernote, and Todoist

Analysis of US customer reviews reveals distinct differences in how users experience the paywalls and subscription models of Evernote, Todoist, and Notion. Evernote reviews show a high frequency of keyword matches for pricing and subscription terms, driven by severe backlash over steep price increases and free-tier restrictions. ## Quantitative Keyword-Match Analysis

A keyword query for 'subscribe', 'paywall', 'price', 'free', and 'limit' matched 30 out of 100 sampled US reviews for Evernote. [S29](/api/reports/4679c1260f49484a9b05360a54fd899a/sources/S29)

The same keyword query matched 7 out of 100 sampled US reviews for Todoist. [S30](/api/reports/4679c1260f49484a9b05360a54fd899a/sources/S30)

For Notion, the keyword query matched 4 out of 100 sampled US reviews. [S28](/api/reports/4679c1260f49484a9b05360a54fd899a/sources/S28)

## Recurring Subscription and Paywall Complaints

Low-rated Evernote reviews also complain about aggressive upselling, noting that intrusive promotional videos and subscription pop-ups disrupt basic note-taking. [S9](/api/reports/4679c1260f49484a9b05360a54fd899a/sources/S9) [S32](/api/reports/4679c1260f49484a9b05360a54fd899a/sources/S32) [S35](/api/reports/4679c1260f49484a9b05360a54fd899a/sources/S35)

Todoist complaints focus on feature gatekeeping, such as locking basic task reminders behind a paid tier, and the lack of Apple Family Sharing support on Pro subscriptions. [S21](/api/reports/4679c1260f49484a9b05360a54fd899a/sources/S21) [S26](/api/reports/4679c1260f49484a9b05360a54fd899a/sources/S26)

Notion complaints in the sample highlight billing and subscription glitches, such as paying $119.99 for Notion Plus through Apple but experiencing conflicting in-app subscription statuses. [S33](/api/reports/4679c1260f49484a9b05360a54fd899a/sources/S33)

## What Higher-Rated Reviews Value

Highly-rated Notion reviews emphasize deep workspace customizability and express appreciation for a generous free tier that remains highly accessible to casual users. [S40](/api/reports/4679c1260f49484a9b05360a54fd899a/sources/S40) [S52](/api/reports/4679c1260f49484a9b05360a54fd899a/sources/S52)

## What the data cannot establish

- The analysis is limited to a bounded sample of 100 reviews per app from the US App Store storefront.
- Keyword-match counts represent exact token matches within the sample and do not reflect total semantic complaint prevalence across the entire user base.
- User reviews reflect subjective personal experiences and cannot independently verify developer billing systems or actual corporate pricing structures.

## Questions to investigate next

- Conduct a broader semantic analysis across a larger historical dataset of reviews to track how sentiment shifted immediately following specific pricing updates.
- Investigate the technical reliability of App Store subscription syncing for Notion to determine if billing status errors are widespread.
